# 🪐 CITY GRID MATRIX NAVIGATION PROMPT

## MASTER PROMPT
Create a futuristic Mars city grid matrix navigation UI.

## RULES
- Must be abstract, vector-style, and diagrammatic.
- Do NOT make it a realistic 3D environment render.
- Keep the background clean, dark, and translucent to fit glassmorphic overlays.

## STYLE
- **High-Tech Systems Interface**: Sleek, vector-based network diagram.
- **Glassmorphic & Luminescent**: Semi-transparent backing panel with soft glowing borders and cyan/emerald/amber neon traces.
- **Advanced Civic Graphic**: Feels like a high-end civic transit coordinate board.

## STRUCTURE
- **Sectors represented as glowing nodes** (elegant hexagons, circles, or diamond indicators).
- **Corridors or transit lines** represented as bright, flowing high-speed pathway vectors.
- **Metropolis Core** clearly highlighted as the central operational hub.
- **Active Sector** dynamically pulsing with a premium luminescent state indicator.

## INTERACTION CONCEPT
- Clicking a node initiates a spatial warp transition, routing the citizen to that sector.
- The matrix represents the geographic and systemic network of Ares City.
